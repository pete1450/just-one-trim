Tending your bonsai

Press and hold a branch for a second to prune it there. A ring charges while the piece glows white, so nothing gets snipped by accident. The cut piece tumbles down and fades — the tree doesn't mind. It heals over and pushes out fresh growth, sometimes in surprising directions. That's how you shape it. A snipped leaf puff just grows a tiny twig nub or leafs right back out.

Your tree keeps living while you're away. Open it in the morning and watch it catch up on the night's growth.

The slider sets the pace of its life: slow for a patient tree, fast to watch it change shape while you look.

Nothing here can die. Prune boldly.
